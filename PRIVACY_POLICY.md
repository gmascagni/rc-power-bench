# Privacy Policy for RC Power Bench Pro

**Last updated:** November 8, 2026

**RC Power Bench Pro** ("the Application") is an interactive RC aircraft brushless propulsion calculator, aerodynamic wing loading studio, and avionics telemetry configurator developed by Gabriel Mascagni.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
RC Power Bench Pro does not collect, harvest, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). All user-created aircraft configurations, custom fleet models, battery/ESC/motor setups, and calculation benchmarks are processed locally within your device and persisted solely via local device storage (`localStorage`).

### 2. Permissions
The Application operates with standard web permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, GPS location, device contacts, or external file storage. Optional Web Audio API features synthesize motor/propeller frequencies locally on the device in real-time and do not record or transmit audio.

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
The Application contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or background telemetry services.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
